How Much Does It Cost to Paint a House in Texas?
The cost to paint a house in Texas typically ranges from $2,500 to $8,500, depending on the size of your home, whether you're painting the interior, exterior, or both, and the amount of prep work required. Smaller homes may cost less than $2,000, while large or two-story Texas homes can exceed $12,000 when combining interior and exterior work. What Factors Affect Painting Costs in Texas?
Several Texas-specific factors influence your final price:
UV & Sun Exposure — South- and west-facing walls bake in Texas sun. UV-resistant acrylic paint costs more but stretches repaint cycles to 5–7 years.
Humidity & Gulf Coast Moisture — Houston and East Texas humidity slows drying and can cause blistering if timing is wrong.
Hurricane & Storm Zones — Coastal Texas homes often need elastomeric or premium acrylic-latex paint, adding $0.50–$1.00/sq ft.
Stucco & Brick Prep — Common in San Antonio, Austin, and El Paso. Stucco adds $0.40–$1.00/sq ft; painted brick needs masonry primer.
Home Height & Layout — Two-story Texas homes add roughly 50% to exterior labor.
HOA Color Rules — Many Texas HOAs require pre-approved colors; factor in 2–4 weeks of approval delay.
Best Time of Year to Paint a House in Texas
Timing your project right in Texas can save 5–15% on labor and improve paint longevity.
Best seasons: Late fall (October–November) and early spring (March–April) — mild temps and low humidity.
Avoid: June through August. Surface temps above 120°F cause paint to dry before bonding, leading to cracks and peeling.
Winter is the interior off-season — December through February is the discount window for interior painting cost Texas.
How Do I Estimate My Texas House Painting Cost?
You can estimate your Texas painting cost in three simple steps:
Measure square footage (interior living space or exterior surface area)
Multiply by average cost per sq ft — $1.50–$4.00 exterior, $2.00–$6.00 interior
Add prep, repairs, and paint upgrades — power washing, stucco prep, two-story surcharge
Example: A 2,200 sq ft two-story Dallas home × $2.50/sq ft = $5,500 base, +$1,800 two-story surcharge, +$350 power wash = ~$7,650 total.

FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions About Texas House Painting Costs
How much does it cost to paint a house in Texas? The cost to paint a house in Texas typically ranges from $2,500 to $8,500, depending on home size, project scope, and city. Combined interior and exterior projects can exceed $12,000.
How much does it cost to paint a 2,000 sq ft house in Texas? A 2,000 sq ft Texas home costs $3,000–$6,500 for exterior painting and $4,000–$12,000 for full interior repainting, depending on prep and paint grade.
Is it cheaper to paint a house in Texas than in other states? Generally yes — Texas labor rates sit near or slightly below the national average, with San Antonio and El Paso among the most affordable major markets. Austin is the exception, running about 11% above the national average.
How much do painters charge per hour in Texas? Most Texas painters charge $35–$75 per hour. Two-person crews bill $70–$150 per hour combined, though most projects are quoted by the job.
What is the best time of year to paint a house in Texas? Late fall (October–November) and early spring (March–April) are ideal. Avoid June through August, when extreme heat causes paint to dry too fast and crack.
How long does exterior paint last in Texas? Quality UV-resistant acrylic paint lasts 5–7 years on south- and west-facing Texas walls and 7–10 years on shaded sides. Coastal homes with elastomeric paint typically last 4–6 years.
How much does it cost to paint a brick or stucco house in Texas? Painting brick or stucco in Texas adds $0.40–$1.00 per sq ft for prep and masonry primer. A 2,000 sq ft stucco home typically costs $4,000–$8,500 for exterior painting.
Does painting a house increase its value in Texas? Yes. A fresh exterior paint job returns 50–100% of its cost at resale in most Texas markets and improves curb appeal. Neutral, HOA-approved colors deliver the strongest ROI.
Do I need HOA approval to paint my house in Texas? If your home is in an HOA, yes — most Texas HOAs require written approval of exterior paint colors. Submit swatches early; approval typically takes 2–4 weeks.
How much does it cost to paint the interior of a house in Texas? Interior painting cost Texas runs $2,000–$6,000 for a typical home, or $2.00–$6.00 per square foot. Dallas averages $2,257 and Austin averages $2,453 for interior projects.
