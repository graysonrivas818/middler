How Much Does It Cost to Paint a House in Georgia?
The cost to paint a house in Georgia typically ranges from $1,500 to $10,500, depending on whether you're painting the interior, exterior, or both, your home's size, and how much prep work the job needs. Prices also shift across the state: Atlanta quotes usually run higher than Macon, Augusta, or rural South Georgia because Metro Atlanta's cost of living sits about 8% above the national average. Humidity, red clay dust, and heavy spring pollen all push Georgia painting costs in ways a national average can't capture. What Factors Affect House Painting Cost in Georgia?
1. Georgia's Humidity & Climate
Georgia averages over 70% relative humidity. That slows drying and encourages mildew, which is why many homeowners in Savannah, Brunswick, and coastal areas upgrade to mildew-resistant paint. That upgrade alone can add 15–25% to your paint material cost.
2. Paint Quality
Standard latex runs $25–$35 per gallon, premium runs $45–$65, and high-gloss can hit $70. Premium paints cost more upfront but last longer in Georgia's heat and sun.
3. Surface & Prep Work
Red clay dust and spring pollen mean every exterior job needs a pressure wash, typically $0.20–$0.50 per sq ft. Drywall repair averages around $600 per project, and significant prep can add 30–50% to the total.
4. Home Size, Height & Layout
A two-story home in Alpharetta or Johns Creek adds about 50% per extra story. Complex trim, detailed ceilings, and high foyers all push the Georgia painter cost per square foot higher.
5. Location Within Georgia
Metro Atlanta runs about 8% above the national cost-of-living average, while the rest of Georgia sits around 9% below the national construction-cost average. That gap shows up directly in painter quotes.
6. Season & Timing
Spring and summer are peak season. Expect to book 4–8 weeks out and pay upper-end rates. Late fall and winter can knock 10–20% off interior jobs.
How to Estimate Your Georgia House Painting Cost
You can estimate your painting cost in three simple steps:
Measure your square footage — interior living space for inside jobs, or exterior wall surface area for outside jobs.
Multiply by the local $/sq ft rate — use $2.00–$3.50 for most Georgia interiors, $1.55–$4.25 for Atlanta exteriors.
Add prep, trim, and ceilings — trim runs $1.50–$4.00 per linear foot, ceilings $1.50–$3.50 per sq ft, and accent walls $3–$7 per sq ft.
Worked example: A 2,000 sq ft home in Atlanta × $3.00 per sq ft = $6,000 interior estimate. Add $800 for trim and $400 for minor drywall repairs = ~$7,200 total.

Is It Cheaper to Paint Your Georgia Home Yourself?
DIY painting can cut labor out of the equation, but Georgia's climate makes it risky for bigger jobs.
DIY Pros
Lower upfront cost, since labor is 40–60% of most quotes
Full control over your schedule and color choices
Good fit for small interior rooms and touch-ups
DIY Cons
Georgia humidity can ruin a DIY coat that didn't cure properly, leading to peeling within a year
Two-story Atlanta suburb homes are dangerous for ladder work without proper scaffolding
Spring pollen can contaminate fresh exterior paint and leave a gritty, uneven finish
The time cost is real: a 2,000 sq ft interior can take a weekend painter 40–60 hours
For large homes or exterior projects, hiring professionals is often worth the investment.
Best Time of Year to Paint a House in Georgia
Interior painting is a year-round job in Georgia. Climate control keeps walls dry, and many contractors offer winter discounts of 10–20% on interior projects between December and February.
Exterior painting has a sharper sweet spot. The best windows are March through June and September through November, when temperatures sit between 50°F and 85°F and humidity is manageable. Try to avoid July and August: extreme heat slows drying, pushes paint to dry too fast on the surface, and cuts crew efficiency.
Plan around pollen, too. Atlanta's heaviest pollen weeks hit late March into April. Booking either before or after that stretch helps your exterior finish stay clean while it cures.

Frequently Asked Questions About Painting a House in Georgia
How much does it cost to paint a house in Georgia per square foot?
Interior painting in Georgia runs $1.50–$6.00 per sq ft, and exterior painting runs $1.20–$4.25 per sq ft depending on the city. Atlanta sits at the top end of both ranges, while Columbus and Augusta sit near the bottom.
How much does it cost to paint the interior of a 2,000 sq ft house in Georgia?
The cost to paint interior of house Georgia homes around 2,000 sq ft typically falls between $2,800 and $5,500. Premium paint, ceilings, and detailed trim can push that closer to $6,500.
How much does it cost to paint the exterior of a house in Atlanta, GA?
Exterior house painting cost Georgia averages sit at $3,373 for Atlanta, with a typical range of $2,104–$4,689. Two-story homes and heavy prep can push Atlanta exteriors above $7,000.
Is it more expensive to paint a house in Atlanta than in Macon or Augusta?
Yes. Atlanta full-paint averages run $4,232–$5,565, while Macon runs $3,968–$5,191 and Augusta typically comes in slightly lower. The gap comes from higher Metro Atlanta labor rates and denser demand.
How much do painters charge per hour in Georgia?
Georgia painters charge $40–$100 per hour, with about $42 per hour as the state average for skilled trades. Labor makes up 40–60% of most projects, and up to 70% on labor-heavy exterior jobs.
How long does it take to paint a house in Georgia?
Interior projects usually take 2–5 days, and exteriors take 3–7 days. Humidity can extend exterior timelines in July and August because each coat needs longer to cure.
Does Georgia's humidity affect painting cost or quality?
Yes. Humidity above 70% slows drying and encourages mildew, so many homeowners upgrade to mildew-resistant paint, which adds 15–25% to material cost. Skipping that upgrade in humid areas like Savannah often shortens the life of the job.
What is the best time of year to paint a house in Georgia?
March–June and September–November are the best exterior windows. Interior painting is fine year-round, and winter often brings 10–20% discounts on inside jobs.
How much does it cost to paint a 12×12 room in Georgia?
A 12×12 room in Georgia typically costs $300–$750 for walls only, and $450–$1,000 with trim and ceiling included. Premium paint or heavy drywall repair can push it higher.
Do I need a permit to paint my house in Georgia?
No. Painting the interior or exterior of a single-family home in Georgia does not require a permit. HOA rules in neighborhoods like Alpharetta, Marietta, and Johns Creek often require color approval before you start, though.
Does painting my house in Georgia increase its resale value?
Yes. A fresh paint job can raise home value by 2–5% and sharply improve curb appeal, especially in competitive Atlanta metro markets. Neutral colors and clean trim deliver the best return.
