How Much Does It Cost to Paint a House in Ohio? (2026 Guide)
The cost to paint a house in Ohio typically runs between $1,800 and $8,500+, with most homeowners spending around $3,200 for interior work and $5,400 for exterior work in 2026. Your final price depends on square footage, siding type, how much prep the walls need, and whether you're hiring pros in a metro like Columbus or a smaller town like Marietta. Ohio's older housing stock — century homes in Cleveland, brick builds in Columbus, and historic neighborhoods like German Village and Ohio City — adds real cost, and so does the freeze-thaw climate that chews up exterior paint every winter. Cost to Paint a House in Ohio's Major Cities
Painting cost per square foot in Ohio shifts noticeably between regions. Here's what homeowners are actually paying in 2026.
Columbus
Interior painting in Columbus averages around $1,700 for a standard three-bedroom, with exterior jobs typically running $4,500–$6,000. Older neighborhoods like Clintonville, German Village, and Victorian Village need more prep — plaster walls, lead-safe practices, and detailed trim work push jobs toward the high end.
Cleveland / Northeast Ohio
Cleveland's housing stock is some of the oldest in the state. Painting an aluminum-sided home runs $4,500–$7,750, while century homes in Lakewood, Shaker Heights, or Ohio City typically land between $12,000 and $18,000. Large historic homes on the East Side can hit $19,000–$26,000+ when scaffolding, wood repair, and multi-color Victorian schemes are involved.
Cincinnati
Cincinnati prices mirror Columbus on standard stock but climb fast on hillside homes in Mt. Adams, Hyde Park, and Over-the-Rhine. Victorian-era properties, steep access, and brick repointing routinely push exterior jobs into the $8,000–$15,000 range.
Toledo, Akron, and Dayton
These mid-size metros generally run 8–15% cheaper than Columbus or Cleveland. Expect interior jobs from $1,400–$4,200 and exterior jobs from $2,000–$6,500 for standard homes.
Interior vs. Exterior Painting Costs in Ohio
Cost to Paint Interior of House Ohio
Interior painting in Ohio averages $1.75–$3.50 per square foot, including labor, primer, and two coats of quality paint. Drywall is faster and cheaper to paint than plaster, which is common in pre-war Cleveland, Cincinnati, and Columbus homes and adds 15–25% in labor. Expect to pay $300–$950 per room for a bedroom-sized space. Use the interior painting cost calculator for a room-by-room breakdown.
Cost to Paint Exterior of House Ohio
Exterior work runs $2.25–$4.25 per square foot — more than interior for real reasons specific to Ohio:
Pressure washing is almost always required to strip mildew and chalking
Scaffolding on two- and three-story colonials adds $400–$1,200
Freeze-thaw repair — filling hairline cracks in siding, soffits, and trim
Surface prep varies by siding: vinyl is quickest, wood clapboard needs scraping and priming, brick and cedar demand specialty primers

7 Factors That Affect House Painting Costs in Ohio
🎨 1. Paint Quality
Contractor-grade paint runs $20–$40/gallon. Mid-tier (Sherwin-Williams SuperPaint, Behr Premium) lands at $45–$80. Premium lines like Duration, Aura, and Emerald hit $100+ per gallon but last 10–15 years on Ohio exteriors.
🏠 2. Siding Type
Vinyl is the cheapest surface to paint. Wood clapboard, cedar shake, and brick are the most expensive — brick can add 20–40% because of masonry primer and extra coats.
🧱 3. Home Age & Prep Work
Ohio has one of the oldest housing stocks in the Midwest. Pre-1950 homes add 10–30% due to scraping, caulking, plaster repair, and lead-safe practices.
🪜 4. Number of Stories
Each story adds roughly 50% more in labor. A three-story Cleveland colonial can cost double a single-story ranch of the same square footage.
❄️ 5. Ohio Climate
Freeze-thaw cycles crack caulk and siding every winter. Quality primer and elastomeric paints are non-negotiable on Ohio exteriors and typically add $200–$600 to the material cost.
👷 6. Labor Rates by Region
Ohio painters cost more in Columbus and Cleveland metros, where crew rates run $45–$75/hour, versus $35–$55/hour in rural counties.
⚠️ 7. Lead Paint Remediation
Homes built before 1978 require EPA RRP-certified contractors. Lead-safe work adds $8–$15 per square foot on impacted areas.
How to Estimate Your Ohio House Painting Cost in 3 Steps
You don't need a contractor to ballpark your number. Here's the math painters use.
Measure your paintable square footage. For interiors, multiply perimeter × ceiling height, then subtract windows and doors. For exteriors, multiply wall height × width for each side.
Multiply by Ohio's average $/sqft — use $1.75–$3.50 for interior, $2.25–$4.25 for exterior.
Add 10–30% for prep, repairs, and premium paint. Older homes, brick, and multi-story builds lean toward the higher end.
Worked example: A 2,000 sqft home in Columbus × $2.75/sqft = $5,500 interior estimate. Add 15% for prep on a 1940s Clintonville bungalow and you're at $6,325.

Is It Cheaper to Paint Your Ohio Home Yourself?
DIY can save money — but only on the right project.
DIY pros:
Save 60–75% on labor (labor is typically 70–85% of the total quote)
Full control over paint choice and pace
Reasonable for single rooms, closets, accent walls
DIY cons:
Ohio's tall colonials and three-story Victorians are risky without proper scaffolding
Brick, stucco, and cedar require experience to prep correctly
Winter weather, humidity, and surprise rain can ruin a half-finished exterior
You'll spend $400–$900 on supplies (sprayer, ladders, drop cloths, brushes, primer, paint)
Bottom line: DIY a bedroom or hallway. Hire a pro for any exterior, any brick, and anything over one story. For the honest middle ground, see the national painting cost guide.
When Is the Best Time to Paint a House in Ohio?
Ohio's weather window is real, and painters plan around it.
Best exterior window: late May through early October — dry air, daytime temps 60–85°F, nighttime temps 50°F+.
Avoid: April (too wet), November–March (paint won't cure below 50°F).
Winter interior discounts: Many Ohio painters offer 10–20% off interior work from December through February to keep crews busy.
If you're flexible, schedule interiors in January or February and book exteriors by March for a May–June start.

Frequently Asked Questions About Painting a House in Ohio
How much does it cost to paint a 2,000 sq ft house in Ohio?
Expect $3,500–$7,000 for interior, $4,500–$8,500 for exterior, and $7,500–$14,800 combined for a standard 2,000 sqft Ohio home in 2026. Older homes in Cleveland or Cincinnati push toward the high end because of plaster, prep, and lead-safe requirements.
How much do painters charge per hour in Ohio?
Ohio painters cost $35–$75 per hour, with most independent pros at $45–$55/hour and larger crews in Columbus or Cleveland at $60–$75/hour. Most quote by the job, not the hour, but labor makes up 70–85% of a typical painting quote.
What's the cheapest city in Ohio to paint a house?
Mid-size and rural metros like Toledo, Dayton, Youngstown, and Mansfield are typically the cheapest — often 8–15% less than Columbus or Cleveland thanks to lower labor and overhead costs.
How often should I repaint my house in Ohio?
Plan on repainting the exterior every 5–10 years in Ohio, depending on siding and paint grade. Interiors last 7–12 years, with high-traffic areas like kitchens, hallways, and kids' rooms needing a refresh closer to 5 years.
Do I need a permit to paint my house in Ohio?
No, standard residential painting does not require a permit anywhere in Ohio. You may need EPA RRP-certified contractors if your home was built before 1978, and HOA or historic district approval in neighborhoods like German Village or Ohio City.
How long does it take to paint a house in Ohio?
A typical 2,000 sqft interior takes 3–5 days; an exterior takes 4–7 days weather permitting. Century homes, multi-story colonials, and brick jobs can stretch to 2–3 weeks.
Does painting increase home value in Ohio?
Yes. A fresh exterior paint job returns 50–100% of its cost at resale in most Ohio markets, and interior repaints in neutral colors consistently shorten time-on-market. It's one of the highest-ROI pre-listing moves in Columbus, Cincinnati, and Cleveland.
Is exterior paint more expensive in Ohio than other states?
Ohio exterior paint costs are roughly at or slightly below the national average. Labor is cheaper than coastal states, but Ohio's freeze-thaw climate demands higher-grade paint and primer, which evens out the total.
How much does it cost to paint a brick house in Ohio?
Painting a brick home in Ohio costs $4,500–$12,000+, typically $3.50–$5.25 per square foot. Brick needs masonry primer, two topcoats, and careful prep — budget 20–40% more than you would for vinyl or wood siding.
Can I paint my Ohio home in winter?
You can paint the interior year-round — in fact, it's when most Ohio painters offer discounts. Exterior painting in Ohio winter is not recommended because paint won't cure properly below 50°F.
