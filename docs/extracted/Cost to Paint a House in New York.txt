Cost to Paint a House by Region in New York
Not all of New York pays the same NYC painter prices per square foot. Here's how regional averages stack up:
New York City (all 5 boroughs): $4.25 – $7.25 per sq ft. Highest labor rates in the state, plus COIs, building permits, elevator reservations, and co-op/condo rules that add time and paperwork.
Long Island (Nassau & Suffolk): $3.75 – $6.25 per sq ft. Slightly lower than NYC but still elevated by suburban labor costs and larger home footprints.
Westchester & Hudson Valley: $3.50 – $6.00 per sq ft. Historic homes and higher-end finishes push the average up.
Albany & Capital Region: $2.75 – $4.75 per sq ft. More competitive contractor market.
Buffalo, Rochester & Western NY: $2.50 – $4.50 per sq ft. The most affordable region in the state.
NYC is the most expensive because painters price in union-adjacent labor rates, insurance minimums, parking, gear-hauling for walk-ups, and weekend-only work windows that many buildings enforce.
Interior vs Exterior Painting Costs in New York
Interior Painting Cost New York
Interior painting cost New York projects typically run $3.00 to $6.50 per square foot for walls, ceilings, and trim. Co-op and condo interiors often require licensed, insured painters with current Certificates of Insurance on file with the building. That narrows your contractor pool and slightly raises prices. Pre-war apartments with plaster walls, crown molding, and tall ceilings also cost more than a straightforward drywall repaint. For room-by-room pricing, use the Interior Painting Cost Calculator.
Exterior Painting Cost New York
Exterior painting cost New York projects run $3.50 to $7.25 per square foot on average. Brownstone façades, shingle siding, and multi-story Victorians in places like Park Slope or Saratoga Springs sit at the top. Winter weather shortens the exterior painting window to roughly April through October, so scheduling gets tight and peak-season pricing kicks in by late spring. What Factors Affect House Painting Costs in New York?
Paint Quality. Premium paints (Benjamin Moore Aura, Sherwin-Williams Emerald) add $30 to $50 per gallon but last longer and cover better.
Surface Condition. Peeling paint, water damage, or plaster cracks add prep hours. NYC pre-war units almost always need extra prep.
Home Height & Layout. Walk-ups, brownstones, and multi-story homes need ladders, scaffolding, or lifts, which raise labor costs 15% to 30%.
NY Labor Rates. NYC painters typically charge $55 to $95 per hour. Upstate crews run $35 to $60 per hour.
Prep Work. Sanding, patching, priming, and trim masking can account for 30% to 50% of total project time.
Building & Co-op Requirements. Many NYC buildings require COIs naming the building as an additional insured, elevator reservations, weekend-only work windows, and floor protection. Each requirement adds time and cost.
How to Estimate Your New York House Painting Cost
You can get close to a real New York house painting estimate in three steps:
Measure total paintable square footage. For interiors, multiply wall length by ceiling height and subtract doors and windows. For exteriors, measure siding area.
Multiply by a NY-specific rate. Use $3.00 to $6.50 per sq ft for interior, $3.50 to $7.25 per sq ft for exterior.
Add prep, repairs, and premium paint. Budget an extra 10% to 25% for patching, priming, and higher-end finishes.
Worked example: A 1,800 sq ft Brooklyn rowhouse interior at $3.25 per sq ft comes out to $5,850. Add $600 in prep and a second coat on the stairwell, and you're at roughly $6,450 before tax.

Is It Cheaper to Paint Your New York Home Yourself?
DIY can cut costs, but New York adds friction most other states don't.
Pros:
Save 50% to 70% on labor.
Control paint quality and schedule.
Good fit for single rooms, small exteriors, or rental prep.
Cons:
NYC co-ops and condos often require licensed, insured painters with COIs on file. That usually rules out DIY.
Prep work on older NY homes (plaster, lead paint risk in pre-1978 buildings) is time-consuming and regulated.
Ladders and scaffolding for brownstones and multi-story exteriors are dangerous without experience.
No warranty on your own work.
For a single bedroom, DIY makes sense. For a full interior, a brownstone exterior, or anything in a co-op, hiring a pro is usually worth it.

Frequently Asked Questions About Painting Costs in New York
How much does it cost to paint a house in New York?
The cost to paint a house in New York typically ranges from $3,200 to $18,500+. Most homeowners spend $6,500 to $12,000 for a combined interior and exterior job on a mid-sized home.
How much does it cost to paint a house per square foot in NYC?
NYC painter prices per square foot run about $4.25 to $7.25 for most homes, depending on prep work, ceiling height, and building access rules.
Why is house painting more expensive in New York?
Higher labor rates, insurance minimums, COIs, parking and gear-hauling time, co-op and condo rules, and weekend-only work windows all push NY prices 20% to 40% above the national average.
How much does it cost to paint a 2,000 sq ft house in New York?
A 2,000 sq ft home typically costs $5,500 to $10,500 for interior painting and $7,000 to $12,800 for exterior painting in New York.
How much do painters charge per hour in New York?
NYC painters usually charge $55 to $95 per hour. Upstate NY painters typically charge $35 to $60 per hour.
Do I need a licensed painter in NYC?
New York State does not license painters, but NYC co-ops, condos, and many rental buildings require licensed, insured contractors with a valid Certificate of Insurance. Always check your building's rules first.
How long does it take to paint a house in New York?
A single room takes one to two days. A full interior on a 2,000 sq ft home takes three to five days. A full exterior typically takes four to seven days, weather permitting.
Is it cheaper to paint a house in winter in New York?
Interior painting can be slightly cheaper in winter because demand drops. Exterior painting is tough from November through March in New York because of temperature and moisture, so most pros won't quote it off-season.
How much does it cost to paint a brownstone?
Painting a brownstone exterior in NYC typically costs $8,000 to $20,000+, depending on height, trim detail, and whether the façade needs pointing or repairs before paint.
Does painting increase home value in New York?
Yes. A fresh interior and exterior paint job typically returns 50% to 100%+ of its cost at resale in NY markets, and it's one of the highest-ROI prep moves before listing.
