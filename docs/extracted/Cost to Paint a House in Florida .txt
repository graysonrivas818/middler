Cost to Paint a House in Florida: 2026 Price Guide

Most Florida homeowners pay between $3,000 and $10,000 to paint a 1,500–3,000 sq ft house for a single scope (interior or exterior). Combine both and the typical Florida house painting cost lands between $7,000 and $18,000, depending on square footage, siding type, and city.
Florida prices run a little higher than the national average because of UV exposure, humidity, stucco prep, and hurricane-season scheduling. Below we break it down by size, city, and job type, then you can run your own numbers in the Middler paint estimator.

Why Painting a House in Florida Costs More Than the National Average
Florida is one of the toughest paint environments in the U.S. Five reasons your estimate runs higher than a comparable home in Ohio or Tennessee:
1. UV intensity. Direct sun fades and chalks paint fast. A Florida exterior often needs repainting every 3–5 years, versus 5–7 years nationally.
2. Humidity and mildew. High dew points feed mildew growth on shaded elevations. Mildew-resistant paints and bleach washdowns are standard, not optional.
3. Coastal salt air. Homes within a few miles of the coast — Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Sarasota, Jacksonville Beach — get more aggressive oxidation, which means more prep and premium coatings.
4. Hurricane season (June–November). Crews compress bookings into the dry months, and last-minute schedule shuffles around storms add cost.
5. Stucco-heavy housing stock. Most Florida homes are stucco or block with stucco finish, which often needs elastomeric paint to bridge hairline cracks. Elastomeric adds roughly $1–$2 per sq ft over a standard acrylic system.

Factors That Affect Your Florida House Painting Cost
Paint quality
Builder-grade paint runs $20–$40/gallon and covers about 350–400 sq ft. Premium Florida-ready lines like Sherwin-Williams Emerald or Benjamin Moore Aura land at $60–$100+/gallon and deliver the UV and mildew resistance you actually want on a Florida exterior.
Surface condition
Peeling paint, chalky stucco, wood rot around fascia, and failed caulking all add labor hours. Heavy prep can add 10–30% to the total.
Home height and layout
Two-story homes, vaulted entryways, and gables over pool cages require ladders, scaffolding, or lifts. Expect a 20–40% bump over a single-story of the same footprint.
City and location
Coastal metros (Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Naples, Sarasota) cost more per sq ft than inland markets (Gainesville, Ocala, Lakeland). Labor, insurance, and permitting all trend up on the coast.
Prep work
Power washing, stucco crack repair, caulking, priming bare spots, and scraping flaking wood are the core prep items on a Florida exterior. On interiors, drywall patching and ceiling stain blocking are the usual add-ons.
Siding type
Stucco — most common; often needs elastomeric. $2.50–$4.50/sq ft.
Wood siding / Hardie plank — benefits from premium acrylic. $2.00–$4.00/sq ft.
Vinyl — requires vinyl-safe paint. $1.75–$3.50/sq ft.
Fiber cement — takes paint well, minimal prep. $2.00–$3.75/sq ft.

Best Time of Year to Paint a House in Florida
The best window for exterior painting in Florida is late fall through early spring — roughly November through April. Humidity drops, afternoon storms stop, and temperatures sit comfortably in the 60s–80s.
Avoid July through September. Daily thunderstorms, 90%+ humidity, and active hurricane tracks push crews off schedule and stress wet paint film. Interior work can happen year-round since your AC controls the environment.

DIY vs Hiring a Pro in Florida
DIY pros

Full control of schedule and color choices
DIY cons
Two-story Florida exteriors require real ladder and safety gear
Stucco prep (patching, texturing, elastomeric application) is skilled work
Mistakes on elastomeric or mildew-prone areas will show within a year
Florida-specific callouts
Licensing and insurance matter. Florida requires painting contractors to carry liability and workers’ comp. Hiring unlicensed help during hurricane season can leave you exposed if a ladder accident or storm-damage dispute happens.
Stucco is not beginner work. Hairline cracks, bridging, and two-coat elastomeric systems are easy to botch.
Permits. Some HOAs and coastal municipalities require color approval or permits for exterior repaints.

For most 2,000+ sq ft Florida homes, a pro crew is the sensible call. DIY makes sense for a single interior room or a small condo.

How to Estimate Your Florida House Painting Cost
You can ballpark your number in three steps:
Measure your interior living space (for interior) or exterior wall surface area (for exterior).
Multiply by the Florida average — use $2.50/sq ft as a middle-of-the-road interior number or $3.00/sq ft for exterior stucco.
Add prep costs — 10–30% for power wash, crack repair, caulking, and priming. Add $1–$2/sq ft more if you’re using elastomeric on stucco.
Example: A 2,000 sq ft Orlando exterior × $3.00/sq ft = $6,000, + $1,200 prep = ~$7,200.

Frequently Asked Questions About House Painting Costs in Florida
How much does it cost to paint a house in Florida?
The average cost to paint a house in Florida is $3,000–$10,000 for one scope (interior or exterior) and $7,000–$18,000 for a combined repaint of a 1,500–3,000 sq ft home. Prices vary by city, siding type, and prep requirements.
How much to paint the exterior of a house in Florida?
Exterior painting cost in Florida runs $1.25–$4.50 per sq ft, or roughly $3,000–$10,000 for a typical single-story home. Two-story homes, coastal locations, and elastomeric coatings on stucco push the number higher.
How much to paint the interior of a 2,000 sq ft house in Florida?
Expect $4,000–$9,000 for a 2,000 sq ft Florida interior with two coats, standard prep, and mid-grade paint. High ceilings, trim work, and premium paint add to the total.
Why is painting a house in Florida more expensive?
Florida house painting prices are higher because of intense UV, humidity, salt air, and stucco-heavy construction. Paint fails faster (3–5 years vs 5–7 nationally), so premium UV- and mildew-resistant coatings are standard.
How often should I repaint my house in Florida?
Most Florida exteriors need repainting every 3–5 years. Coastal homes on the low end of that range, inland homes with shaded elevations on the high end. Interiors typically go 5–8 years between repaints.
What’s the best paint for Florida’s climate?
Look for 100% acrylic exterior paints with UV blockers and mildewcide. Sherwin-Williams Emerald, Behr Marquee, and Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior are common Florida picks. For stucco, elastomeric is the go-to.
Do I need elastomeric paint for stucco in Florida?
For most aging stucco homes, yes. Elastomeric bridges hairline cracks and blocks wind-driven rain — a big deal during hurricane season. It costs about $1–$2 more per sq ft than standard acrylic but typically lasts longer on stucco.
When is the best time of year to paint a house in Florida?
Late fall through early spring (November–April) is ideal. Humidity is lower, storms are rare, and temperatures are paint-friendly. Avoid July–September when daily storms and hurricane activity peak.
Does a fresh paint job increase home value in Florida?
Yes. Exterior paint delivers roughly 55% ROI, and paint combined with landscaping can return up to 152% ROI on pre-sale homes, according to Zillow’s 2026 data. In Florida’s curb-appeal-driven coastal markets, a clean repaint is one of the highest-impact pre-listing moves.
How much does it cost per square foot to paint a house in Florida?
Interior runs $2–$6 per sq ft. Exterior runs $1.25–$4.50 per sq ft. Add $1–$2 per sq ft for elastomeric coatings on stucco.
Is it cheaper to paint a house in Jacksonville or Miami?
Jacksonville is typically 15–25% cheaper than Miami for the same scope. Miami labor rates, coastal prep, and permitting costs all run higher.
